Vaultio

A private vault for your photos, videos, files and notes. Encrypted on your iPhone, with no account and no tracking.

Coming soon to the App Store.

What it does

Bring things in

Add photos and videos from your library, files from the Files app, or take new photos and videos straight into the vault. Write private notes too.

View them in place

Photos with zoom and swipe, videos, PDFs, text and notes open inside Vaultio. Filter by type, or search by name.

Tidy up your library

After importing from Photos or Files, Vaultio can delete the originals for you, so the private copy is the only one left on your iPhone.

How it protects you

  • Encrypted on your iPhone. Every item and its name are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using Apple's built-in cryptography.
  • Keys only you hold. Your vault opens with your 6-digit passcode, with Face ID if you turn it on, or with your 24-character Recovery Key. Your passcode works together with a key held in this iPhone's Secure Enclave.
  • Kept out of sight. Vault contents don't appear in the Files app, Spotlight search or iPhone backups. The vault is hidden in the App Switcher and while your screen is recorded or mirrored, and it locks when you leave the app.
  • Nothing leaves your iPhone. Vaultio has no account, no ads, no analytics, no third-party code, and no networking code at all.

Good to know before you start

  • Your vault lives on this iPhone only. If you lose or replace your iPhone, the vault can't be recovered. Encrypted iCloud backup is planned for a future update.
  • Keep your Recovery Key safe. If you forget your passcode, it's the only way back in. Nobody, including us, can reset it for you.
  • Exported copies aren't protected. Anything you share or save back to Photos is an ordinary, unencrypted copy.
  • Screenshots can't be blocked. iOS doesn't let any app prevent them.
  • iPhone only for now. Vaultio requires iOS 18 or later.

Pricing

Vaultio is free for up to 50 items, with every feature included. Vaultio Plus removes the limit:

US prices. Prices in other countries and regions are set by the App Store and may include tax. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel, and you can cancel any time in your Apple Account settings. If your subscription ends, you can still open, view, export and delete everything in your vault.

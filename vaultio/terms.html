Free use

You can use every feature of Vaultio for free with up to 50 items in your vault.

Vaultio Plus

Vaultio Plus removes the 50-item limit. It's available as:

  • Vaultio Plus Monthly: an auto-renewable subscription, renewing every month.
  • Vaultio Plus Yearly: an auto-renewable subscription, renewing every year.
  • Vaultio Plus Lifetime: a one-time purchase that doesn't renew.

Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy, and may vary by country or region.

Subscriptions

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Your subscription renews automatically at the same price and for the same period, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings (on iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions), or in Vaultio under Settings > Subscription > Manage Subscription. If you cancel, Vaultio Plus stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • If Apple offers a free trial or introductory price and you don't cancel before it ends, the subscription continues at the regular price.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies. See how to request a refund.

Lifetime purchase

Vaultio Plus Lifetime is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. You can restore it on any iPhone signed in to that account with "Restore Purchases" in Vaultio's settings.

If Vaultio Plus ends

You never lose access to your own data. You can still unlock your vault, view, export and delete everything in it. You just can't add new items while you're above the free limit.

Your passcode, Recovery Key and data

  • Your vault is encrypted with keys only you hold. We can't open it, recover it, or reset your passcode.
  • If you forget your passcode, your Recovery Key is the only way back in. If you lose both, the contents of your vault can't be recovered by anyone.
  • In Vaultio 1.0 your vault is stored only on your iPhone and isn't included in iCloud or computer backups. If your iPhone is lost, stolen, damaged, erased or replaced, the vault can't be recovered.
  • Exported or shared copies of your items are ordinary, unencrypted files outside Vaultio's protection.
  • Please keep your own copies of anything you can't afford to lose.

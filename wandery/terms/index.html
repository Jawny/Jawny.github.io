A record of days, not advice

  • Wandery records where you were and counts days against thresholds you choose or that a rule describes. It is not tax, immigration, legal or financial advice, and it doesn't tell you whether you are a resident of any place, whether you qualify for a visa, residence or citizenship, or what tax you owe.
  • Each rule follows our reading of its official source, which the app names. Laws, forms and official guidance change, have exceptions, and are applied by authorities to your particular facts. A count in Wandery is not a ruling by any authority.
  • Please check with a qualified professional before making decisions based on a count, and keep your own records (such as travel documents and receipts) where an authority may ask for them.

How complete the record is

  • Days come from your iPhone's location events, the date and place saved with your photos, boarding passes you scan, and days you enter. Any of these can be missing or wrong: iOS may not wake the app, a short visit may be missed, a photo's location may be someone else's, and borders are drawn from public map data that is simplified and may differ from official boundaries.
  • Wandery shows days it can't determine as unknown rather than guessing, shows inferred days as "likely", and shows how each day was recorded, so you can check and correct them. You're responsible for checking the days that matter to you.
  • Evidence PDFs and CSV exports summarize what Wandery recorded. They aren't certified records.

Free use

You can use Wandery for free for the current calendar year and the previous one: automatic counting, the photo scan, the map, trips, stats, day counts, Year in Review, the today widget and CSV export of those years. Older years found by the photo scan are kept and shown, dimmed, and are never deleted because you haven't bought Wandery Plus.

Your data

  • Your records are stored on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud database. We never receive them and can't recover them.

  • "Delete All Data" in Wandery's Settings permanently deletes your records on that iPhone and, through iCloud, on your other devices.

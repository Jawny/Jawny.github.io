Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026. This policy applies to Web Streams for AirPlay version 1.0 for iPhone, its Safari extension (Streams), and this website.

  • Web Streams collects no data. No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code, no account, and no server of our own.
  • What you watch, and where, never leaves your phone. The extension looks at a page only to find its video, and only on the sites you turn it on for.
  • The video goes from the website to your TV, or to the app's player, the same way it goes to Safari. Nothing passes through us.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal data when you buy Web Streams.

Who we are

Web Streams for AirPlay is made by Johnny Liao, an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have a question about this policy or your privacy, email wumbusapps@gmail.com.

What the app collects

Nothing. Web Streams doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share any information about you or about how you use it. In particular, the app and its extension have:

  • no account or sign-up;
  • no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software, and no third-party code of any kind;
  • no tracking across apps or websites, and no use of the advertising identifier;
  • no server of our own: neither the app nor the extension ever connects to us.

The App Store privacy label for Web Streams is "Data Not Collected".

The Safari extension and your browsing

Streams, the Safari extension, runs only on the websites you turn it on for, or on every website if you choose that. Safari asks for your permission first, and you can take it back at any time in Safari or in Settings.

  • What it reads: on those sites, the video elements on the page and the addresses of the video streams the page itself loads. To recognize a stream it may look at the first few bytes of what the page downloads; it keeps none of it. It needs these to put the TV button on the video and to tell Safari where the stream is.
  • What it changes: nothing, until you tap the TV button. Then it turns AirPlay on for that one video.
  • What it keeps: the list of sites you turned it on for, in Safari's storage for the extension on your phone. It doesn't keep a history of what you watch, and it doesn't store stream addresses.
  • What it sends: nothing. The extension makes no network requests of its own; Safari loads the pages as usual.

Sending a video to your TV

  • With the TV button: Safari hands your TV the stream's address over AirPlay, on your own network, and the TV gets the video from the website, just as when a site offers AirPlay itself.
  • With Play in Streams: the extension passes the stream's address, the page's address and title, and where you were in the video to the Web Streams app, on your phone, after Safari asks you. The app's player then gets the video from the website, and AirPlay sends it to your TV. The app keeps no history of what you played.
  • What the website sees: the website (or its video server) receives the requests for the video, from your TV or your phone, with their IP address, as it does when you watch in Safari. We don't run those servers and receive nothing from them.

You can check this yourself: Settings > Privacy & Security > App Privacy Report lists every server an app contacts.

Purchases

Web Streams is sold by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your name, email address, Apple Account details or payment information. Apple gives us sales reports, such as the number of downloads by country or region, which don't identify you. For refunds, see Support.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen, in your device's settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share crash reports and aggregated usage statistics (such as the number of installs) with us. These are collected by Apple, not by Web Streams, and never include what you watch. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. Reviews you post on the App Store are public and visible to us.

When you contact us

If you email us, including with Email Support in the app (which opens your own Mail app), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as the address of a site that didn't work. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems, we don't share it, and we keep it only as long as we need to. Ask us at any time and we'll delete it.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no scripts, no web fonts and no third-party resources. It's hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We don't have access to those logs.

Children

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (EU and UK) and the CCPA (California) give you rights to access, correct, delete or move personal information about you, and to object to certain uses. Since the app doesn't collect any personal information, the only information we might hold is email you've sent us. To make a request about it, email wumbusapps@gmail.com. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. We don't sell or share personal information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of Web Streams changes what it sends or where, we'll update this policy before that version is released.
